Digital product, design, and strategy for clients across retail, content, marketing, health care, education, children's media, sports, entrepreneurship, not-for-profit, farming, and music. Notable projects:

  • Floorball Canada (floorballreferee.com): Designed, produced, and built an online courseware and referee-management platform that gave the national board a country-wide training solution. Includes a 30-lesson e-learning course (writing, video production and editing), plus user management, tiered payment processing, forums and forms.
  • Misfit Studio: Maintained website and ran business management for a pilates, yoga, and movement studio through its recovery from a devastating flood and its move to a new Queen St West location, while expanding its offerings. Covered operations, accounting, payroll and billing, HR, marketing, and change management.
  • WEMADEIT (Hydro-One & 5 Ontario universities): Led discovery, research, and production of the online components of a rebrand of engineering aimed at increasing female enrolment in engineering programs, including 360 surveys and 700+ peer, parent and teacher interviews. Designed and built the website
  • Scopify (Royal Ontario Museum): Designed a downloadable phone app that augments the museum exhibit experience with augmented reality.
  • Ruby Skye P.I.: digital production, design, and web development for a web series with multichannel story interfaces and Ontario-curriculum-interwoven materials.
  • CBC Kids: product management, design, and development for the What's Your News?! iPhone and Android app.
