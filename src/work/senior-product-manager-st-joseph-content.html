St. Joseph Content produced flyers and catalogues for national retailers. Progressed from Senior Business Analyst (2015–2016) to Senior Product Manager (2016–2018).

  • Sold and led a complete rebuild of the proprietary ECMS platform, from a client-specific Python monolith into a multi-tenant Node.js microservice SaaS product for national retail clients, designed to increase scalability and client lock-in.
  • Led a team that grew from 6 to 10, building the next-generation product while maintaining legacy applications. The team built and maintained apps, APIs, and cloud infrastructure for content production, management, and distribution ecosystems.
  • Owned the product end to end: market research, roadmap, requirements, UX/UI design, sales collateral, SOWs and SLAs, hiring and retention.
  • As Senior Business Analyst, led the build and onboarding of Shoppers Drug Mart's national digital flyer and offer distribution program, including API integrations with upstream and downstream systems and vendors.
  • Delivered analytic driven recommendations for Canon Canada's canonfeatures.ca, reducing bounce rate by 50% and improving conversions by 200%.
